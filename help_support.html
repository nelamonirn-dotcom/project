Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a train ticket? +

To book a train ticket:

  1. Visit the home page and search for trains by selecting origin and destination
  2. Choose your preferred train and date
  3. Select your desired seats
  4. Proceed to payment using credit/debit card or mobile payment services
  5. Receive your e-ticket confirmation via email
What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept multiple payment methods including:

  • Debit/Credit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, AMEX, Nexus
  • Mobile Financial Services: bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, Tap
  • Internet Banking: DBBL and other banks
Can I cancel or modify my booking? +

Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking up to 24 hours before departure. The refund process:

  • Login to your account
  • Go to "My Bookings"
  • Select the ticket and choose "Cancel" or "Modify"
  • Cancellation charges may apply
  • Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days
How far in advance can I book tickets? +

You can book train tickets up to 10 days in advance. This allows you to plan your journey ahead while ensuring availability and ensuring timely processing of your booking.

What if my payment fails? +

If your payment fails:

  • Check your account balance and card status
  • Ensure correct payment details are entered
  • Try alternative payment method
  • Contact your bank if card is blocked
  • Any deducted amount will be refunded within 8 business days
What are the different class types available? +

AC Berth (AC-B): AC cabin berth for nighttime travel

AC Seat (AC-S): AC cabin seat for daytime travel

Snigdha: Premium AC chair car

First Class Berth: Non-AC cabin berth for nighttime

First Class Seat: Non-AC cabin seat for daytime

Shovan Chair: Non-AC chair car

Shovan: Non-AC flat bench seat

How do I download my e-ticket? +

Your e-ticket will be sent to your registered email address. You can also:

  • Login to your account
  • Go to "Purchase History" or "My Bookings"
  • Click on the booking to view ticket details
  • Click "Download Ticket" to save PDF
  • Check your spam/junk folder if email not received
What should I do if I lose my ticket? +

If you lose your physical or e-ticket:

  • Login to your account and download the ticket again
  • Contact support with your booking reference number
  • Bring a valid ID to the station counter for verification
  • You can board the train with proper identification matching the booking
