E-Ticket Issuance

E-tickets will be automatically issued by Bangladesh Railway Portal within 30 minutes of successful payment. User(s) can obtain their ticket(s) and also download the PDF version of the ticket from the confirmation page.

Email Delivery: Concurrently, a copy of the ticket will be sent to the user's email address. However, some mail service providers such as Gmail, Yahoo!, Outlook might place the email in "SPAM" folder. Furthermore, it might also be possible that the mail has been blocked by any of the mentioned service providers. Email containing PDF tickets may be delayed for technical reasons.

Important: Unavailability of the confirmation mail should not be regarded as non-issuance of ticket. User(s) can always download the tickets from the "Purchase History" section of the website or "My Tickets" section of Rail Sheba app.

Ticket Purchase Limitations Per Day

General Purchase Limits:
  • Maximum 4 tickets (seats) can be purchased in a single transaction (online or at station counters)
  • Maximum 2 transactions are allowed per day
  • Maximum of 8 tickets can be purchased per day
  • Train numbers must be different for the two transactions
  • Origin stations must be different for the two transactions
  • Destination stations must be different for the two transactions
Eid Journey Restrictions:

Journey Dates (23rd May 2026 to 28th May 2026): Users can do only 1 transaction and purchase maximum 4 seats.

Return Journey Dates (29th May 2026 to 4th June 2026): Users can do only 1 transaction and purchase maximum 4 seats. Eid tickets are non-refundable.

OTP Delivery Notice:

OTP delivery may be delayed due to congestion at telco or SMS Gateway's end. Shohoz-Synesis-Vincen JV is not responsible for any delay in OTP delivery.

Payment & Security

To issue railway tickets, Bangladesh Railway Portal depends on different payment gateways for the Card/Wallet charge information. Bangladesh Railway does not store any sensitive information of Passengers such as Card/Wallet details, OTP, or Pin Code.

Payment Failure & Card Charging:

In case any Card/Wallet is charged and/or Payment Gateway fails to return the information in due time, it is possible that the passenger's Card/Wallet would be charged without being issued for the desired tickets.

In such instances, Payment Gateways will automatically refund the purchased amount to their respective Card/Wallet within 8 (eight) working days.

Refund Support:
  • If you do not receive refund within 8 working days, send a complaint email to support@eticket.railway.gov.bd with details of the issue
  • A reply will be sent within 7 working days
  • For refund of unsuccessful purchases and card charging issues, contact the Payment Service Provider through which you made the transaction
  • For refund of successfully purchased tickets, visit your respective originating station and contact the refund counter

Note: Bangladesh Railway or Shohoz-Synesis-Vincen JV will not be held responsible for the non-issuance of tickets or delay of refund due to errors in payment processing or any other reasons beyond their control. Bangladesh Railway makes no guarantee/warranty that any of the above services will be uninterrupted, timely or error-free.

Ticket Refund Policy

Domestic Tickets

Time Frame Before Departure Deduction Amount
48 hours or more BDT 40 or 10% of ticket fare (whichever is more)
Less than 48 hours and more than 24 hours BDT 40 or 25% of ticket fare (whichever is more)
Less than 24 hours and more than 12 hours BDT 40 or 50% of ticket fare (whichever is more)
Less than 12 hours and more than 6 hours BDT 40 or 75% of ticket fare (whichever is more)
Less than 6 hours No refund

Service charges for online purchase are non-refundable.

Refund Timeline: The refund amount will be disbursed to your respective MFS (bKash, Nagad, Rocket) or bank card (VISA / MasterCard / Nexus) within 8 business days. However, refunds to bank cards may take longer and may vary according to your card issuer bank's refund policies.

International Tickets

Time Frame Before Departure Deduction Amount
120 hours or more AC Class: BDT 40 | First Class: BDT 20 | Other Classes: BDT 15
Less than 120 hours and more than 96 hours 50% of ticket fare
Less than 96 hours and more than 72 hours 75% of ticket fare
Less than 72 hours No refund

Ticket Purchase, Refund and Journey Rules & Regulations

Key Rules:
  1. Non-Transferable: This ticket is non-transferable and non-assignable
  2. Children Tickets: It is mandatory for children between 3 to 12 years old to purchase minor tickets
  3. Luggage Allowance: No additional fees for passengers who travel within the luggage weight limits of:
    • AC: 56 KG
    • First Class: 37.5 KG
    • Shovon Chair / Shovon: 28 KG
    • Shulobh: 23 KG
  4. Coach/Seat Changes: The coach/seat number may change during the journey due to unavoidable circumstances
  5. Valid Travel Document: For traveling with Bangladesh Railway, the passenger must have a valid ticket. No expired ticket or ticket with a future date of travel would be valid
  6. Ticket Verification: It is the responsibility of the Client-Passenger to check the correctness of the ticket concerning the travel date and time, destination, seat number and coach details
  7. Wrongly Purchased Tickets: Wrongly purchased tickets may be replaced depending on the availability of the desired destination, seat number etc.
  8. Legal Requirement: Do not travel by Bangladesh Railway without purchasing a ticket. Any traveling without a valid ticket may be subject to prosecution
  9. Liability: Except for whatever liability is admitted by Bangladesh Railway or Shohoz-Synesis-Vincen JV in these terms and conditions, neither Bangladesh Railway nor Shohoz-Synesis-Vincen JV accepts any liability arising out of the rail ticket or the travel

Complaints & Grievances

E-Ticket & Website Issues:

In case of any concerns or grievance against tickets purchased through the e-ticket website or Rail Sheba app, a passenger should notify Bangladesh Railway within 2 hours by sending an email along with the e-ticket details to support@eticket.railway.gov.bd

Other Grievances:

For any other grievance including tickets purchased from counters or any sort of inconvenience caused at stations or trains during travel, passengers are requested to raise their complaints to Bangladesh Railway through the concerned Traveling Ticket Examiner ("TT"). Any such complaint must be supported by the corresponding tickets.

Such grievances will be strictly regulated by Bangladesh Railways Act, 1890 (Act IX of 1890) and Bangladesh Railway Coaching Tariff.

Provisions for Cases in Which Tickets Have Been Issued for Trains Not Having Room Available

  1. Acceptance Condition: Fares shall be deemed to be accepted, and tickets to be issued, subject to the condition of there being room available on the train for which the tickets are issued
  2. Refund on No Availability: A person to whom a ticket has been issued and for whom there is not room available in the train for which the ticket was issued shall on returning the ticket within three hours after the departure of the train be entitled to have his fare refunded at once
  3. Downgrade Refund: A person for whom there is not room available for the class of carriage for which he has purchased a ticket and who is obliged to travel in a carriage of a lower class shall be entitled on delivering up his ticket to a refund of the difference between the fare paid by him and the fare payable for the class of carriage in which he traveled
